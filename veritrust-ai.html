psychology The Engineering Challenge
"How do enterprises deploy generative customer-support LLMs without risking contractual hallucination, false pricing promises, or compliance breaches?"

While LLMs are gifted conversationalists, their probabilistic nature means they will occasionally fabricate favorable refund dates, combine discount codes incorrectly, or invent policies out of thin air. In customer-support environments, a single hallucinated pledge can create legal liability or heavy chargebacks.

lightbulb
The Solution Architecture A decoupled Maker-Judge topology. The Maker produces candidate drafts; the Judge acts as an immutable firewall. The Judge splits drafts into atomic propositions, compares them to ground-truth ChromaDB vector chunks, and routes arithmetic calculations to a deterministic code engine.
